Stainless Steel Investment Castings for Bathroom and Sanitary Ware Applications

A practical guide for OEM buyers and product engineers evaluating custom metal components.

Bathroom and sanitary ware products may use custom metal components where geometry, material selection, surface finish and fit with adjoining parts matter. Stainless steel investment casting is one manufacturing route buyers can evaluate for suitable component designs.

Where custom cast components may be considered

Depending on the design and project requirements, investment-cast stainless steel parts may be considered for selected bathroom hardware, sanitary ware fittings, trim or support components, and other OEM metal parts. This is not a fixed product catalog: suitability depends on the drawing, intended function, material specification, operating environment and required finishing or machining.

Why evaluate investment casting?

  • Complex geometry: Casting may be suitable for shapes that are difficult or costly to produce from stock by machining alone.
  • Material options: Stainless steel grades should be selected against corrosion conditions, strength, appearance and applicable project standards.
  • Secondary operations: Machining, polishing or other finishing may be required to meet the final design and assembly requirements.
  • Project-specific validation: Dimensions, tolerances, surface finish and inspection criteria should be agreed from the engineering specification.

What should buyers provide for an RFQ?

To help a manufacturer assess feasibility and prepare a useful quotation, buyers should provide as much of the following as available:

  • 2D engineering drawing and/or 3D CAD model
  • Material grade or performance requirements
  • Estimated annual volume and initial order quantity
  • Critical dimensions, tolerances and inspection requirements
  • Surface finish, polishing and machining requirements
  • Application and service environment
  • Target sampling schedule and delivery destination
