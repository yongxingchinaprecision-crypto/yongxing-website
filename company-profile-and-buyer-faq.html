What does Yongxing Precision manufacture?

Yongxing Precision focuses on custom precision cast metal components made through silica sol investment casting. The company works with stainless steel, carbon steel, and alloy steel. Components may be considered for bathroom and sanitary ware applications, depending on the part geometry, material specification, finish, and project requirements.

Manufacturing and quality information

The company profile identifies investment casting equipment introduced from Taiwan and a Spectrocast spectrometer among its equipment. The company has stated annual precision casting output of more than 1,000 metric tons. These details should be verified and supported with current records before being used in a formal buyer qualification package.

Achievable dimensions, tolerances, surface roughness, secondary machining, polishing, inspection methods, and acceptance criteria are project-specific. Buyers should confirm them using the latest drawing and written specifications rather than relying on a general website statement.

What should a U.S. buyer include in an RFQ?

Include the latest 2D drawing and, if available, a 3D model; material grade and standard; annual and initial order quantities; required finish; critical dimensions and tolerances; inspection or certification requirements; packaging needs; and target delivery schedule.

Can Yongxing confirm tolerances and surface finish before an order?

These are best confirmed during technical review. Please identify critical dimensions, datum references, surface requirements, and inspection criteria on the drawing or specification so the supplier can assess the project accurately.
