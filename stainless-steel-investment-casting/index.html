About Yongxing Precision Casting

Yongxing Precision Casting Co., Ltd. is a precision casting manufacturer serving customers who require custom metal components. Our production focuses on silica sol investment casting for parts designed around customer drawings and technical specifications.

We work with stainless steel, carbon steel and alloy steel. Our applications include bathroom and sanitary ware-related components as well as other industrial casting requirements.

Our Silica Sol Investment Casting Process

Silica sol investment casting is used to manufacture metal parts from a designed pattern and ceramic shell. The suitable process and inspection plan depend on the part geometry, material, dimensions and customer requirements.

Our factory uses investment casting equipment introduced from Taiwan. Technical requirements are reviewed against the drawings and specifications supplied for each project.
