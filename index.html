05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions from purchasing teams.

What manufacturing process do you use?

Our company profile describes a full silica sol investment casting process. Share your drawing and requirements so we can assess whether the process suits your component.

Which materials can you discuss?

We work with stainless steel, carbon steel and alloy steel investment casting projects, subject to the part specification and technical review.

Can you manufacture from our drawing or existing mold?

Yes, you can send a technical drawing or details of existing tooling. We will review the requirements before confirming feasibility, tooling needs and quotation.

Do you support bathroom and sanitary ware components?

We have experience with bathroom and sanitary ware related metal components. Please send drawings or reference photos so we can understand the specific part.

What information should we include in an inquiry?

Include drawings, material grade, dimensions and tolerances, surface finish, estimated quantity and any special inspection requirements.

Can you confirm price and lead time immediately?

Price and lead time depend on the design, material, tooling, quantity and finishing requirements. We review the project information before providing a quotation.
